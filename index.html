About Us
Natyoga Darshanam
Integrative movement discipline
Yoga (union and awareness) + Natya (dance and expression)

NYD is a transformative approach that trains the nervous system, refines expressive depth, and restores joy in artistic embodiment.​Uniting the Ancient sciences, a profound teaching methodology rooted from two foundational texts: ​ Yogasutra of Sage Patanjali and the Natyashastra of Bharatamuni.

Yoga Sutras of Patanjali 1.3

तदा द्रष्टुः स्वरूपेऽवस्थानम्

Tadā draṣṭuḥ svarūpe’vasthānam

Meaning

Then, the Seer abides in its own true nature.

When the dancer goes beyond technique and ego, what remains is pure presence. The performer disappears, and only the experience of truth remains.

Founder
Rajadarshini Saravanan

As the first in her bloodline to pursue Bharatanatyam, her journey has been shaped by deep dedication and divine grace & the blessings of her Guru. Trained intensively under the renowned Dr. Smt. Sheela Unnikrishnan at Sridevi Nrithyalaya in Melattur Bani Bharatanatyam, Rajadarshini has been a part of over 300 production performances, captivating audiences worldwide with her grace, precision, and profound understanding of classical Indian dance traditions.

Rajadarshini Saravanan is an internationally acclaimed Bharatanatyam dancer, yoga practitioner, movement researcher, and educator, with over two decades of rigorous training and performance experience, and more than ten years of teaching across India, Europe, Asia, the UAE, the United Kingdom, the USA, and East Africa.

Vision

To establish Natyoga Darshanam as a globally recognised integrative movement institution that harmonises yoga, classical dance, and embodied awareness, nurturing injury-free, emotionally intelligent, and spiritually aligned dancers who experience stillness within dynamic movement.

Movement Training Methodology

The Natyoga Movement Architecture

Rooted in the foundational wisdom of the Yato Hasta Tato Drishti principle from the Natyashastra by Bharata Muni, Natyoga recognises that movement is not mechanical, but conscious:​

यतो हस्तस्ततो दृष्टिर्

यतो दृष्टिस्ततो मनः।

यतो मनस्ततो भावो

यतो भावस्ततो रसः॥

Yato Hastas Tato Drishti,

Yato Drishti Tato Manaha |

Yato Manas Tato Bhavo,

Yato Bhava Tato Rasaha ||

Where the hand moves, the eyes follow.​
Where the eyes move, the mind follows.​
Where the mind moves, emotion awakens.​
Where emotion awakens, rasa is experienced.​
